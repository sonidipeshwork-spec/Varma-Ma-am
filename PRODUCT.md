# For Shruu

A private, single-page love letter. Design is the gift.

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Users
One person: Shruu, reading on a phone or laptop, likely at night.

## Purpose
Let her move through a curated sequence of why she is loved—portraits, stages of falling, a letter dated 28 May 2026, and a promise—without it feeling like a template “love landing page.”

## Personality
Intimate, jewel-box, stereoscopic. Physical object: a gold-stamped locket that opens into a room.

## Anti-references
Cream parchment scrapbooks, heart-emoji rain, identical polaroid grids, glassmorphism everywhere, Cormorant + Dancing Script “romance defaults.”
